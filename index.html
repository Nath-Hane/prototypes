Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using our self-guided plans in Taiwan.

Do I need to download an app from the App Store? ▼

No. Taiwanderer is built as a lightweight, mobile-first Progressive Web App (PWA). It runs directly in your standard mobile browser (Safari, Chrome) with zero installation required.

How do the Traditional Chinese flashcards work? ▼

At every stop (street food stall, taxi transfer, teahouse), a prominent "Show to Local" button opens a high-contrast card with the official Traditional Chinese characters (繁體中文), phonetic pinyin, and clear request phrases.

What is included in the estimated budget? ▼

All budgets are calculated per person in New Taiwan Dollars (NTD). They cover local transport (MRT, EasyCard fares, buses), recommended signature dishes, and admission tickets.
